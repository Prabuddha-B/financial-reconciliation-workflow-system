Upload Guidelines
Supported Files
  • CSV (.csv)
  • Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)
  • One file per upload
Validation Rules
  • Reference No is required for Receipt and Accounting records.
  • Employee Name is required for Payroll records.
  • Vendor Name is required for Stock Purchase records.
  • Amount/Salary must be greater than zero.
  • Stock Purchase Date, Stock Created At, and Accounting Record Date must be valid dates.
  • Rows with parsing errors or failed validations are marked invalid and are not saved.
  • Duplicate IDs/references (such as receipt ID, reference no, purchase ID, invoice no, record ID) are skipped during save.
